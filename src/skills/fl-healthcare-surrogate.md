## Statutory Basis
Florida Statutes §765.101 et seq. (Health Care Advance Directives)
Florida Statutes §765.202 (Designation of Health Care Surrogate)

## Execution Requirements — CRITICAL (§765.202)
- Principal signature
- TWO witnesses:
  * At least ONE witness must NOT be a spouse or blood relative of principal
  * Cannot be the surrogate
  * Each needs: signature + PRINTED NAME
- No notary required by statute — but include for HIPAA authorization
- If principal cannot sign: may direct another to sign in their presence

## Scope of Surrogate Authority
- Consent to or refuse any medical treatment
- Access all medical records (HIPAA — include express authorization)
- Make end-of-life decisions if living will provisions included
- Does NOT cover mental health treatment (separate document required)

## Living Will Provisions — Three Conditions to Address
If included, must address all three:
1. Terminal condition — illness or injury with no reasonable probability of recovery
2. End-stage condition — irreversible condition caused by injury, disease, or illness
3. Persistent vegetative state — permanent and irreversible condition of unconsciousness

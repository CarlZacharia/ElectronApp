# Florida Lady Bird Deed (Enhanced Life Estate Deed)
## Zacharia Frey PLLC — Drafting Skill

---

## Statutory / Legal Basis
Florida common law. No specific codifying statute. Upheld in Blocker v. Blocker and
recognized by Florida title insurers. Avoids probate under F.S. §319.29 for titled 
property when properly drafted.

## What This Deed Does
Transfers remainder interest to named beneficiaries while grantor retains:
1. Lifetime possession and use
2. Right to sell, mortgage, or convey WITHOUT beneficiary consent
3. Right to revoke or change beneficiaries at any time
4. All rents and proceeds during lifetime

## Execution Requirements — CRITICAL
- Grantor signature required
- TWO witnesses required — each witness must have:
  * Signature line
  * PRINTED NAME line (not just signature)
  * ADDRESS line
- Notary acknowledgment with commission expiration date
- Must be recorded in county where property is located

## Homestead Analysis (ALWAYS CHECK)
- If property is homestead AND grantor is married:
  * Spouse must join in the deed even if not on title
  * Failure to get spouse joinder = voidable deed
- If property is homestead AND grantor has minor children:
  * Cannot devise away from minor children
  * Lady Bird deed may not be appropriate — flag for attorney review
- Single grantor with no minor children: no restriction

## Common Issues — Flag These with [REVIEW REQUIRED]
- [ ] Is property homestead? Is grantor married? Minor children?
- [ ] Legal description must be verbatim from prior vesting deed or title search
- [ ] Multiple grantors require each to have separate signature block
- [ ] Out-of-state grantor — notary form may differ
- [ ] Property in an LLC or existing trust — grantor authority must be verified
- [ ] Remainderman predeceases grantor — is there an alternate? Lapse language needed?

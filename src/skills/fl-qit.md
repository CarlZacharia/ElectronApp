# Florida Qualified Income Trust (Miller Trust / QIT)
## Zacharia Frey PLLC — Drafting Skill

---

## Statutory Basis
42 U.S.C. §1396p(d)(4)(B) — Federal enabling statute
Florida Medicaid Policy — Florida Administrative Code Rule 65A-1.712
OBRA '93

## When Required
Required when a Medicaid applicant's gross monthly income EXCEEDS the
Florida income cap (currently $2,901/month for 2024 — verify current figure).
ALL income must be deposited into the QIT each month before paying anything else.

## Critical Rules
- Trust must be irrevocable during beneficiary's lifetime
- Trustee must be someone other than the beneficiary
- Only the beneficiary's income goes into the trust (not assets)
- ALL gross income must be deposited — not just the excess
- Funds flow through the trust monthly — trust does not accumulate
- Upon beneficiary's death, state of Florida is primary remainder beneficiary
  up to total Medicaid paid (estate recovery)
- Trust must be established BEFORE the month of Medicaid application

## Monthly Fund Flow (in order)
1. All gross income deposited to QIT account
2. Personal Needs Allowance paid to beneficiary ($160/month nursing home)
3. Minimum Monthly Maintenance Needs Allowance (MMMNA) to community spouse (if any)
4. Health insurance premiums paid
5. Incurred medical expenses (not covered by Medicaid)
6. Patient Responsibility (cost share) paid to nursing facility
7. Balance (if any) remains in trust — should be near zero monthly

## Bank Account Requirements
- Must be a separate dedicated bank account
- Account titled in name of trust
- Trustee must be signatory
- Most Florida banks recognize QIT accounts

## Trustee Selection
- Cannot be the beneficiary
- Typically: adult child, spouse, sibling
- Corporate trustee acceptable but rarely used for QIT
- Trustee has fiduciary duty — stress importance of monthly deposits

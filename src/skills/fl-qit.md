# Florida Qualified Income Trust (Miller Trust / QIT)
## Zacharia Frey PLLC — Drafting Skill

## Statutory Basis
42 U.S.C. §1396p(d)(4)(B) — Federal enabling statute
Florida Medicaid Policy — Florida Administrative Code Rule 65A-1.712
OBRA '93

## When Required
Required when a Medicaid applicant's gross monthly income EXCEEDS the
Florida income cap (currently $2,901/month for 2024 — verify current figure).
ALL income must be deposited into the QIT each month before paying anything else.

## Critical Rules
- Trust must be irrevocable during beneficiary's lifetime
- Trustee must be someone other than the beneficiary
- Only the beneficiary's income goes into the trust (not assets)
- ALL gross income must be deposited — not just the excess
- Funds flow through the trust monthly — trust does not accumulate
- Upon beneficiary's death, state of Florida is primary remainder beneficiary
  up to total Medicaid paid (estate recovery)
- Trust must be established BEFORE the month of Medicaid application

## Monthly Fund Flow (in order)
1. All gross income deposited to QIT account
2. Personal Needs Allowance paid to beneficiary ($160/month nursing home)
3. Minimum Monthly Maintenance Needs Allowance (MMMNA) to community spouse (if any)
4. Health insurance premiums paid
5. Incurred medical expenses (not covered by Medicaid)
6. Patient Responsibility (cost share) paid to nursing facility
7. Balance (if any) remains in trust — should be near zero monthly

## Bank Account Requirements
- Must be a separate dedicated bank account
- Account titled in name of trust
- Trustee must be signatory
- Most Florida banks recognize QIT accounts

## Trustee Selection
- Cannot be the beneficiary
- Typically: adult child, spouse, sibling
- Corporate trustee acceptable but rarely used for QIT
- Trustee has fiduciary duty — stress importance of monthly deposits

QUALIFIED INCOME TRUST
(MILLER TRUST)
FOR FLORIDA MEDICAID LONG-TERM CARE ELIGIBILITY

[BENEFICIARY FULL LEGAL NAME] QUALIFIED INCOME TRUST

Established pursuant to 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(B) and Florida Statutes § 409.9102
THIS QUALIFIED INCOME TRUST (hereinafter "Trust") is created on ______________________, 20____, by and between:
TRUSTOR/BENEFICIARY: [BENEFICIARY FULL LEGAL NAME], a resident of [COUNTY] County, Florida, Social Security No. XXX-XX-[LAST FOUR DIGITS]  (hereinafter "Beneficiary"); and
TRUSTEE: [TRUSTEE FULL LEGAL NAME], residing at [TRUSTEE ADDRESS], [CITY], Florida [ZIP]  (hereinafter "Trustee").
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Beneficiary requires long-term care services in a nursing facility (or other institutional or home and community-based waiver setting) within the State of Florida and intends to apply for or maintain eligibility for Florida Medicaid Institutional Care Program (ICP) benefits; and
WHEREAS, the Beneficiary's gross monthly income exceeds the applicable Florida Medicaid income cap established under Florida Statutes § 409.9102 and applicable federal law (42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(B)), thereby necessitating the establishment of a Qualified Income Trust to achieve and maintain Medicaid eligibility; and
WHEREAS, this Trust is established solely to satisfy the requirements of 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(B) (commonly known as a "Miller Trust" or "Qualified Income Trust") and applicable Florida law, rules, and AHCA/DCF policy;
NOW, THEREFORE, the parties hereby agree as follows:
ARTICLE I NAME AND PURPOSE OF TRUST
1.01  Name. This Trust shall be known as the "[BENEFICIARY FULL LEGAL NAME] Qualified Income Trust."
1.02  Purpose. The sole purpose of this Trust is to hold the income of the Beneficiary in a manner that satisfies the requirements of 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(B) and applicable Florida law so that the Beneficiary may qualify for, obtain, and maintain Florida Medicaid long-term care benefits.  This Trust is not a general asset-protection or estate-planning trust and shall not be used for any purpose other than as set forth herein.
1.03  Governing Law; Medicaid Compliance. This Trust shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida.  The Trustee shall administer this Trust in strict compliance with: (a) 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(B); (b) 42 C.F.R. Part 435; (c) Florida Statutes Chapter 409, including § 409.9102; (d) Florida Administrative Code Rule 65A-1.714; and (e) all applicable Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA) and Department of Children and Families (DCF) policies and guidelines, as they may be amended from time to time.
1.04  Irrevocability. Notwithstanding any other provision herein, once this Trust has been used to establish or maintain Medicaid eligibility, it may not be amended, modified, or revoked in any manner that would cause the Trust to no longer comply with 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(B) or applicable Florida law.  During the Beneficiary's lifetime and Medicaid participation, the Trust shall be irrevocable as to the Beneficiary's interest.
ARTICLE II TRUST CORPUS AND DEPOSITS
2.01  Trust Corpus. The initial corpus of this Trust consists of the sum of Ten Dollars ($10.00) deposited by the Trustee to open the Trust account, which the Trustee hereby acknowledges receipt of.  The corpus of the Trust shall consist solely of the income of the Beneficiary as described herein.
2.02  Required Monthly Deposits. On or before the first business day of each month (or immediately upon receipt), the Trustee, or the Beneficiary's authorized representative, shall deposit into the Trust account ALL of the Beneficiary's gross monthly income from every source, including but not limited to:
A.	Social Security retirement, disability (SSDI), or survivor benefits;
B.	Supplemental Security Income (SSI), if any;
C.	Railroad retirement benefits;
D.	Pension and retirement plan distributions;
E.	Veterans Administration (VA) benefits (excluding aid and attendance allowances paid directly by VA to the facility);
F.	Required minimum distributions from IRAs or other retirement accounts;
G.	Annuity payments;
H.	Any other income counted by Florida Medicaid for purposes of the income cap.
CRITICAL COMPLIANCE NOTE: The entire gross income of the Beneficiary must be deposited into this Trust each month — not merely the amount in excess of the applicable income cap.  Failure to deposit all income into the Trust each month will constitute a disruption of Medicaid eligibility for that month and may result in termination of benefits and personal liability for nursing facility costs.
2.03  No Other Property. No property other than the Beneficiary's income shall be deposited into or held in this Trust.  Assets (i.e., non-income resources), gifts, loans, or inheritances shall not be deposited into this Trust account under any circumstances.
ARTICLE III DISTRIBUTIONS DURING BENEFICIARY'S LIFETIME
3.01  Permissible Distributions — Priority Order. Each month, after receipt of all income deposits, the Trustee shall distribute the Trust funds in the following order of priority, and for no other purpose:
i.	FIRST — Personal Needs Allowance (PNA): The Trustee shall pay to or for the benefit of the Beneficiary the monthly Personal Needs Allowance established by AHCA/DCF (currently $160.00 per month as of 2026, subject to adjustment), to be used solely for the Beneficiary's personal clothing, incidental personal expenses, and similar needs while residing in a nursing facility or other institutional setting.
ii.	SECOND — Health Insurance Premiums: The Trustee shall pay any health insurance premiums owed by the Beneficiary, including Medicare Part B premiums, Medicare supplement (Medigap) insurance premiums, Medicare Advantage plan premiums, or other health insurance premiums approved by AHCA/DCF, to the extent such premiums are not deducted at source and are verified and authorized.
iii.	THIRD — Spousal Monthly Income Allowance (if applicable): If the Beneficiary has a community spouse, the Trustee shall pay to the community spouse the income diversion amount calculated pursuant to Florida Medicaid's Minimum Monthly Maintenance Needs Allowance (MMNA) rules under 42 U.S.C. § 1396r-5 and AHCA policy, as determined and authorized by DCF or AHCA in writing.  The diversion amount shall not exceed the authorized MMNA minus the community spouse's own income, as recalculated each eligibility determination period.
iv.	FOURTH — Dependent Family Member Allowance (if applicable): If authorized by DCF/AHCA, the Trustee shall pay any Family Member Allowance for a dependent child, parent, or sibling of the Beneficiary (or community spouse), to the extent authorized under 42 U.S.C. § 1396r-5(d)(1)(C).
v.	FIFTH — Patient Responsibility (Cost of Care): The Trustee shall pay to the nursing facility (or other authorized Medicaid long-term care provider) the Beneficiary's monthly patient responsibility (also called "cost of care" or "monthly income obligation"), as calculated by AHCA/DCF.  The patient responsibility is equal to the Beneficiary's total gross monthly income, less all deductions set forth in items (i) through (iv) above and as authorized by AHCA/DCF.
vi.	SIXTH — Incurred Medical Expenses: If any balance remains after all of the foregoing distributions, the Trustee may pay incurred medical expenses of the Beneficiary that are not covered by Medicaid, to the extent permitted by AHCA/DCF policy, including deductibles, copayments, or non-covered services.
3.02  No Accumulation; Monthly Distribution. All Trust income must be distributed monthly in the priority order set forth in Section 3.01.  No funds shall be accumulated in the Trust account from month to month, except for the minimum balance required to maintain the bank account and any amounts pending distribution for the current month.  Any unexpended balance remaining at the end of each month shall be applied toward the patient responsibility for that month or the next month, as permitted by AHCA/DCF.
3.03  No Other Distributions. Except as expressly set forth in Section 3.01, no distributions from this Trust shall be made to the Beneficiary, to the Trustee (other than reasonable trustee fees as set forth in Section 5.03), or to any other person or entity.  Any distribution not authorized by 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(B), Florida Statutes § 409.9102, and applicable AHCA/DCF policy shall be void and shall constitute a ground for termination of this Trust.
ARTICLE IV RESIDUAL DISTRIBUTION — STATE OF FLORIDA AS REMAINDER BENEFICIARY
4.01  State of Florida — Primary Remainder Beneficiary. Upon the death of the Beneficiary, the State of Florida, through AHCA, shall be the first remainder beneficiary of this Trust.  AHCA shall be entitled to receive from the remaining Trust assets the total amount of Medicaid benefits paid on behalf of the Beneficiary from the date of the initial Medicaid approval through the date of the Beneficiary's death (the "Medicaid Lien").  This provision is mandatory and may not be modified, waived, or reduced by any party.
4.02  Notice of Death. The Trustee (or successor trustee, personal representative, or authorized agent) shall, within thirty (30) days of the Beneficiary's death, provide written notice of the Beneficiary's death to AHCA's Estate Recovery Unit at the address then designated by AHCA.
4.03  Distribution of Remaining Balance. After full satisfaction of the State of Florida's Medicaid claim pursuant to Section 4.01, any remaining Trust balance (if any) shall be distributed as follows:
[ ] To the Beneficiary's estate, to be administered under the Beneficiary's Last Will and Testament or the laws of intestacy of the State of Florida;  OR

   Name: ______________________________________  Relationship: ______________________
   Name: ______________________________________  Relationship: ______________________

(Strike or complete one option above at the time of execution.)
4.04  Sufficiency of Remaining Assets. In the event the remaining Trust assets are insufficient to fully satisfy the State of Florida's Medicaid claim, no beneficiary other than the State of Florida shall receive any distribution from this Trust, and no other person shall be personally liable for the insufficiency solely by reason of this Trust.
ARTICLE V TRUSTEE
5.01  Initial Trustee. The initial Trustee of this Trust shall be [TRUSTEE FULL LEGAL NAME].  The Trustee shall not be the Beneficiary.  If the Beneficiary lacks legal capacity and has no legally authorized representative other than themselves, a competent adult must be appointed as Trustee.
5.02  Successor Trustee. If the initial Trustee is unable or unwilling to serve, resigns, dies, or becomes legally incapacitated, the following person(s) shall serve as Successor Trustee in the order listed:
   First Successor Trustee: ______________________________________
   Second Successor Trustee: ______________________________________
5.03  Trustee Compensation. The Trustee shall serve without compensation unless otherwise authorized by court order or applicable law.  Reasonable and documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred in the administration of this Trust may be reimbursed from Trust assets, provided such expenses do not reduce the funds available for distributions required by Section 3.01 above.
5.04  Trustee Powers. Solely to the extent necessary to administer this Trust in accordance with its stated purposes, the Trustee shall have the following powers:
I.	To open and maintain a separate, dedicated bank account in the name of this Trust at a federally insured financial institution;
J.	To receive, hold, and disburse the Beneficiary's income deposited into the Trust account in strict accordance with Article III;
K.	To maintain complete and accurate records of all deposits, disbursements, and account balances, and to make such records available to AHCA, DCF, or their authorized agents upon request;
L.	To execute checks, wire transfers, or electronic payments in furtherance of the distributions required by Article III;
M.	To file any required tax returns on behalf of the Trust; and
N.	To take any other ministerial action required to administer this Trust in compliance with applicable law.
5.05  Trustee Limitations. The Trustee shall have no power to: (a) invest Trust assets other than in the Trust bank account; (b) commingle Trust assets with any other funds; (c) make distributions not authorized under Article III; (d) amend, modify, or revoke the provisions of this Trust required by 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(B) or applicable Florida law; or (e) take any action that would jeopardize the Beneficiary's Medicaid eligibility.
5.06  Separate Account Required. The Trustee shall open and maintain a dedicated checking or savings account in the name of this Trust.  The Trust account shall not be commingled with any personal or other accounts of the Trustee or the Beneficiary.  The account title shall reflect the Trust name and identify the Trustee as trustee.
ARTICLE VI MEDICAID COMPLIANCE AND REPORTING
6.01  AHCA and DCF Access. The Trustee shall, upon request and without delay, provide to AHCA, DCF, or any of their authorized agents: (a) complete copies of all Trust account statements; (b) documentation of all deposits and disbursements; (c) a complete copy of this Trust instrument; and (d) any other information requested in connection with a Medicaid eligibility determination, redetermination, or audit.
6.02  Changes in Income. The Trustee and/or the Beneficiary's authorized representative shall promptly notify DCF (within ten (10) days or as otherwise required by applicable rules) of any change in the Beneficiary's income amount or income sources that affects the calculation of the patient responsibility or the required Trust deposit amount.
6.03  Annual Redetermination. The Trustee shall cooperate fully in the annual Medicaid eligibility redetermination process, including timely submission of all requested documentation.
6.04  Medicaid Agency Approval. This Trust shall be submitted to DCF/AHCA for approval as part of the Beneficiary's Medicaid application or redetermination.  The Trust shall be effective for Medicaid purposes only upon acceptance by DCF/AHCA.
ARTICLE VII TERMINATION OF TRUST
7.01  Grounds for Termination. This Trust shall terminate upon the earliest to occur of the following events:
O.	The death of the Beneficiary;
P.	The Beneficiary's permanent termination of eligibility for and participation in Florida Medicaid long-term care benefits (other than temporary suspension);
Q.	A final order of a court of competent jurisdiction directing termination; or
R.	Termination required by applicable law or AHCA/DCF directive.
7.02  Distribution Upon Termination. Upon termination of this Trust, the Trustee shall distribute the remaining Trust assets in accordance with Article IV, subject first and always to full satisfaction of the State of Florida's Medicaid claim.
ARTICLE VIII MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
8.01  Severability. If any provision of this Trust is determined by a court of competent jurisdiction or by AHCA/DCF to be inconsistent with the requirements of 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(B) or applicable Florida law, such provision shall be deemed null and void, and the remaining provisions of this Trust shall remain in full force and effect.  The Trustee shall promptly amend the Trust to cure any such inconsistency.
8.02  Amendment. This Trust may be amended only to the extent necessary to comply with changes in applicable federal or Florida Medicaid law or AHCA/DCF policy, and only upon written approval of AHCA/DCF, or by order of a court of competent jurisdiction.  No amendment shall be made that would reduce or eliminate the State of Florida's remainder beneficiary status or that would otherwise violate 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(B).
8.03  Tax Treatment. This Trust is intended to be treated as a grantor trust for federal income tax purposes pursuant to Internal Revenue Code §§ 671–679 to the extent applicable.  All income of the Trust shall be reported on the Beneficiary's individual income tax return.  The Trustee shall obtain a Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN) for the Trust and shall file any required tax returns.  The Trustee is advised to consult with a qualified tax advisor regarding the tax implications of this Trust.
8.04  Spendthrift Provision. No interest of the Beneficiary in this Trust shall be subject to anticipation, assignment, encumbrance, pledge, or the claims of creditors of the Beneficiary, except as required by applicable law or AHCA/DCF policy.
8.05  Counterparts. This Trust Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.  A notarized copy of this Trust instrument shall be submitted to DCF/AHCA with the Medicaid application or redetermination documents.
8.06  Construction. This Trust instrument shall be construed so as to effectuate Medicaid eligibility for the Beneficiary in accordance with applicable federal and Florida law.  In the event of any conflict between the terms of this Trust and the requirements of applicable law, the requirements of applicable law shall control.

QUALIFIED INCOME TRUST — TRUSTEE INSTRUCTIONS

MONTHLY CHECKLIST FOR TRUSTEE
Each month, the Trustee must complete the following steps IN ORDER:  Failure to complete any step may cause loss of Medicaid benefits.
STEP 1 — DEPOSIT ALL INCOME (by the 1st of each month, or upon receipt):
Deposit the Beneficiary's ENTIRE gross income into the QIT bank account.  Do NOT deposit only the amount over the income cap.  Do NOT net out Medicare or other deductions before depositing.  If income arrives on different dates, deposit each payment promptly upon receipt.
STEP 2 — PAY PERSONAL NEEDS ALLOWANCE:
Pay $160.00 (or current PNA amount) to the Beneficiary (or to the facility for the Beneficiary's personal account).
STEP 3 — PAY HEALTH INSURANCE PREMIUMS:
Pay any Medicare Part B, supplement, or other authorized premiums.  (Note: Medicare Part B is usually deducted from Social Security at source — verify before making a duplicate payment.)
STEP 4 — PAY SPOUSAL ALLOWANCE (if applicable):
Pay the authorized MMNA income diversion to the community spouse, as authorized in writing by DCF/AHCA.
STEP 5 — PAY PATIENT RESPONSIBILITY TO FACILITY:
Pay the remaining balance (patient responsibility) to the nursing facility (or other authorized provider) by the date required under the facility's agreement.  Obtain a written receipt or confirmation.
STEP 6 — RECORD KEEPING:
Save all bank statements, deposit receipts, and payment confirmations.  Keep records for at least five (5) years after the Beneficiary's death, or longer if requested by AHCA.

IMPORTANT WARNINGS
WARNING 1 — Missed Deposit:  If any income is NOT deposited into the QIT in a given month, the Beneficiary will NOT be Medicaid-eligible for that month, and the full facility cost may become the Beneficiary's personal liability.
WARNING 2 — Unauthorized Distributions:  Distributions from the QIT for any purpose not listed in Article III of the Trust (and not authorized in writing by DCF/AHCA) can constitute fraud, may trigger Medicaid termination, and may require repayment.
WARNING 3 — Change in Income:  If the Beneficiary's income changes (new award, death of spouse, benefit adjustment, etc.), notify the supervising attorney and DCF immediately.  The patient responsibility and deposit amounts must be recalculated.
WARNING 4 — Death of Beneficiary:  Notify the attorney and AHCA's Estate Recovery Unit in writing within 30 days of the Beneficiary's death.  Do NOT distribute any remaining Trust funds until AHCA's Medicaid lien has been satisfied.
WARNING 5 — Do Not Commingle:  The QIT bank account must be used ONLY for this Trust.  Do not deposit personal funds, gifts, or other assets into the QIT account.

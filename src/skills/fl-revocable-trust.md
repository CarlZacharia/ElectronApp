## Statutory Basis
Florida Trust Code — Florida Statutes Chapter 736
Florida Statute §736.0402 (Requirements for creation)
Florida Statute §736.0602 (Revocation or amendment)

## Purpose and Advantages
- Avoids probate for all properly funded trust assets
- Provides privacy (not a public record unlike a will)
- Allows for incapacity management without guardianship
- Seamless multi-state asset administration
- Does NOT avoid Florida estate tax (none currently) or federal estate tax

## Trustee Succession
Standard succession for single person trust:
1. Grantor/Trustee (during life and competency)
2. Successor Trustee (upon incapacity or death)
3. Second Successor Trustee (backup)
Trustee has full power to act without court supervision.

## Homestead Property in Trust
- Florida homestead can be transferred to a revocable trust — §689.071
- Trust must be a "land trust" or meet specific requirements
- Homestead exemption is preserved if grantor is primary beneficiary
- Portability for ad valorem tax purposes preserved
- Lady Bird deed to trust is common alternative — flag for discussion

## Pour-Over Will
A pour-over will MUST be drafted simultaneously:
- Catches any assets not transferred to trust during life
- Names trust as residuary beneficiary
- Must be executed with full will formalities

## Trust Funding — Critical
Trust is useless unless funded. Standard assets to retitle:
- Real property (deed into trust — Lady Bird deed for homestead)
- Bank accounts (POD or retitle)
- Investment/brokerage accounts (retitle or TOD)
- Business interests (assignment)
- Personal property (assignment of personal property)

Assets NOT transferred to trust (pass by other means):
- IRAs/401(k)s — beneficiary designation
- Life insurance — beneficiary designation
- Jointly held property — survivorship

## Distribution Provisions
At grantor's death, trust distributes to named beneficiaries.
Common structures:
- Outright distribution at death
- Age-based distribution (e.g. 1/3 at 25, 1/3 at 30, balance at 35)
- Discretionary trust for beneficiary with special needs
- Continuing trust for minor children

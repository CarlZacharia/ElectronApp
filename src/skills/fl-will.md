## Statutory Basis
Florida Statutes Chapter 732 (Probate Code — Intestate Succession and Wills)
Florida Statutes Chapter 733 (Probate Code — Administration of Estates)

## Execution Requirements — CRITICAL (§732.502)
- Testator must sign at the END of the will
- TWO witnesses present simultaneously with testator
- Witnesses must sign in testator's presence and in each other's presence
- Each witness: signature + PRINTED NAME + ADDRESS
- Self-proving affidavit (notarized) — STRONGLY recommended
  * Eliminates need for witness testimony in probate
  * Testator + witnesses all sign before notary simultaneously

## Homestead Devises — CRITICAL RESTRICTIONS (§732.4015)
- Cannot devise homestead away from spouse if married
- Cannot devise homestead away from minor children
- If married with no minor children: can devise to spouse freely
- If unmarried with no minor children: can devise freely
- ALWAYS identify homestead property and flag restrictions

## Elective Share (§732.2065)
- Surviving spouse entitled to 30% of elective estate
- Even if will leaves spouse less
- Flag if client is married and leaving spouse less than 30%

## Pretermitted Spouse (§732.301)
- Spouse married AFTER will executed gets intestate share
- Unless will was made in contemplation of marriage
- Flag if client recently married or planning to marry

## Pretermitted Child (§732.302)
- Child born or adopted AFTER will executed gets intestate share
- Unless omission was intentional (must appear from will)
- Flag if client has or may have children after execution

## Personal Representative Requirements (§733.302-.303)
- Must be Florida resident OR
- Related to testator by blood, marriage, or adoption (any state)
- Corporate PR must be authorized to act in Florida
- Flag if named PR is non-resident non-relative

## Common Issues — Flag with [REVIEW REQUIRED]
- [ ] Homestead property — is testator married? Minor children?

- [ ] Is there a pour-over to a revocable trust? Trust must exist.

- [ ] Minor beneficiaries — who manages their inheritance?
- [ ] Are there disabled beneficiaries needing special needs trust?
- [ ] Digital assets clause included?
- [ ] No-contest clause desired?

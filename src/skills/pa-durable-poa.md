## Statutory Basis
Pennsylvania POA Act — 20 Pa. C.S. §§5601-5612
Substantially revised effective January 1, 2015

## Critical 2015 Act Changes
- Mandatory Notice to Principal (verbatim statutory language required)
- Mandatory Acknowledgment by Agent (signed before notary)
- Witness requirements changed
- "Durable" is now the DEFAULT — no longer need express durable language
  but best practice is to include it

## Execution Requirements — CRITICAL
- Principal signature (or direction to sign if unable)
- TWO witnesses:
  * Cannot be the agent
  * Cannot be the agent's relatives
  * Cannot be the principal's care provider
  * Each needs: signature + printed name
- Notary acknowledgment of principal's signature
- Agent must sign Acknowledgment (separate — can be signed later)
- Notice to Principal MUST appear on the first page or immediately before signature

## Mandatory Notice to Principal (§5601(b))
The following notice must appear VERBATIM:

"NOTICE TO THE PRINCIPAL: As the "Principal," you are using this Power of Attorney 
to grant power to another person (called the "Agent" or "Attorney-in-Fact") to make 
decisions about your money and property or, in limited circumstances, your minor 
child and to act for you in all financial and property matters. Read this document 
CAREFULLY. If you do not understand this document, then you should seek legal advice 
before you sign it..."

## Powers Requiring Express Grant (§5601.3)
These CANNOT be implied and must be explicitly stated:
- Create, amend, revoke or terminate an inter vivos trust
- Make a gift (specify annual limit — suggest $18,000/year per donee or less)
- Create or change survivorship interests
- Delegate authority to another person
- Waive right as beneficiary of joint and survivor annuity or retirement plan
- Exercise fiduciary powers

## Gifting Authority — Special Care
- If granting gift authority, must specify limits
- Consider whether gifts to agent should be permitted
- Consider Medicaid implications of gift authority
- Recommend limiting to annual exclusion amount ($18,000 for 2024)

## Agent Acknowledgment (§5601(c)) — REQUIRED
Agent must sign acknowledgment accepting duties BEFORE exercising authority.
Best practice: sign simultaneously with POA execution.

## Statutory Basis
20 Pa. C.S. §§2501-2516 (Wills Act)
20 Pa. C.S. §§3101-3395 (Probate, Estates and Fiduciaries Code)

## Execution Requirements — CRITICAL (20 Pa. C.S. §2502)
- Testator must be 18+ years old and of sound mind
- Testator signature at the END of the will
  (or testator's name written by another at testator's express direction and in presence)
- TWO witnesses who sign in testator's presence
  * Witnesses do NOT need to sign in each other's presence (unlike FL)
  * No address requirement for witnesses (unlike FL)
  * Interested witnesses are valid but creates presumption of undue influence
- Notary NOT required — but self-proving affidavit strongly recommended
  * Self-proving affidavit eliminates need for witness testimony at probate
  * Testator + witnesses all sign before notary

## Holographic Will (20 Pa. C.S. §2502(b))
- Entirely handwritten and signed by testator — valid without witnesses
- Not recommended — advise against

## Pennsylvania Inheritance Tax — CRITICAL (72 P.S. §9101 et seq.)
Tax applies to ALL Pennsylvania residents' estates:
- Spouse: 0%
- Lineal descendants (children, grandchildren): 4.5%
- Lineal ancestors (parents, grandparents): 4.5%
- Siblings: 12%
- All others: 15%
- Charities/government: 0%
Returns due 9 months after death. 5% discount if paid within 3 months.

## Elective Share (20 Pa. C.S. §2203)
Surviving spouse entitled to 1/3 of decedent's estate.
Flag if client is leaving spouse less than 1/3.

## Personal Representative (Executor) Requirements
- No residency requirement in Pennsylvania (unlike Florida)
- Non-resident executor is acceptable
- Corporate executor must be authorized to act in Pennsylvania

## Minor Beneficiaries
If minor receives property under will:
- Property held until age 18 (or 21 if UTMA designation) without trust
- Recommend testamentary trust or UTMA designation for minors

## Specific Issues for PA Practice
- Property held jointly with right of survivorship passes outside will
- POD/TOD accounts pass outside will
- Life insurance passes outside will
- PA does NOT have homestead exemption concerns in the same way as FL

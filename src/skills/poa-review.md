# Power of Attorney Review and Analysis
## Zacharia Frey PLLC — Analysis Skill

---

## Purpose
Analyze an existing General or Durable Power of Attorney for legal sufficiency,
scope of powers, execution defects, and adequacy for client's current needs.

## Analysis Framework

### 1. Document Type and Jurisdiction
- Financial POA vs. Healthcare POA (different analysis)
- Governing jurisdiction
- Is it durable? (Must survive incapacity — check language)
- Is it springing? (FL: NOT permitted post-2011. PA: was permitted, caution)

### 2. Execution Formalities
FL (§709.2105): principal + 2 witnesses (with addresses) + notary + agent acceptance
PA (20 Pa. C.S. §5601): principal + 2 witnesses + notary + notice to principal + agent acknowledgment

### 3. Scope of Powers — What Is Included
Enumerate all powers actually granted. Check for:
- Real property authority
- Banking/financial authority
- Business authority
- Gift-giving authority (must be explicit in both FL and PA)
- Trust authority (must be explicit)
- Retirement plan authority
- Government benefits / Medicaid planning authority

### 4. Scope of Powers — What Is Missing
Identify gaps based on client's current situation:
- No Medicaid planning authority? (Critical for elder law clients)
- No gift authority? (May prevent Medicaid spend-down planning)
- No trust authority?
- No authority to change beneficiary designations?

### 5. Agent Analysis
- Who is the agent? Appropriate choice?
- Is there a successor agent?
- Can co-agents act independently or must they act jointly?
- Can agent self-deal? Are there conflicts of interest?

### 6. Age and Currency
- When was it executed? Is it more than 5 years old?
- Pre-2011 FL POAs may not comply with current Chapter 709
- Pre-2015 PA POAs may not have required notice language
- Some financial institutions refuse older POAs — note if so

### 7. Red Flags
- [ ] Springing POA in Florida post-2011 (invalid)
- [ ] No agent acceptance/acknowledgment in FL
- [ ] No statutory notice in PA
- [ ] Witnesses are agent or agent's relatives (prohibited)
- [ ] Gift authority not explicit but gifts needed for Medicaid planning
- [ ] No Medicaid planning authority for elder law client
- [ ] POA predates major legal reforms

## Output Format
1. DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION — type, jurisdiction, date, parties
2. EXECUTION ANALYSIS — valid? defects?
3. POWERS SUMMARY — what is and is not included
4. ISSUES — numbered, with severity (Critical/Major/Minor)
5. RECOMMENDATIONS
6. QUESTIONS FOR CLIENT

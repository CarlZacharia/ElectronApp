# Trust Review and Analysis
## Zacharia Frey PLLC — Analysis Skill

---

## Purpose
Analyze an existing revocable or irrevocable trust for legal sufficiency,
administrative adequacy, funding status, and planning gaps.

## Analysis Framework

### 1. Trust Type and Jurisdiction
- Revocable living trust vs. irrevocable trust
- Governing law (look for governing law clause)
- Single vs. joint trust
- Date of execution — has law changed?

### 2. Trust Validity (§736.0402 FL / 20 Pa. C.S. §7731 PA)
- Was it properly executed?
- Is there a valid trust purpose?
- Are beneficiaries identifiable?
- Is trust property described?

### 3. Trustee Provisions
- Who is the current trustee?
- Successor trustee succession — is it adequate?
- Trustee powers — are they broad enough?
- How is trustee incapacity determined?
- Trustee compensation provisions
- Is bond waived?

### 4. Beneficiary Provisions
- Who are the current and remainder beneficiaries?
- Are distributions mandatory or discretionary?
- Are there spendthrift provisions? (protects against creditors)
- Are there special needs beneficiaries needing protective provisions?
- Are there minor beneficiaries? Age of distribution?

### 5. Funding Analysis
- What assets are actually in the trust? (Check the schedule of assets)
- Is real property titled in trust name?
- Are financial accounts titled to trust or have POD to trust?
- What is OUTSIDE the trust that should be inside?
- Is there a pour-over will to catch unfunded assets?

### 6. Tax Provisions
- Any A-B trust or credit shelter provisions? (mostly outdated post-ATRA)
- Marital deduction provisions?
- GST (generation-skipping transfer) provisions?
- Income tax — is this a grantor trust? (usually yes for revocable)

### 7. Currency
- When was it drafted? Needs updating if:
  * More than 7-10 years old
  * Major family changes (birth, death, divorce, disability)
  * Major asset changes
  * Move to different state
  * Tax law changes

## Output Format
1. TRUST IDENTIFICATION — type, date, parties, governing law
2. VALIDITY — execution and legal sufficiency
3. TRUSTEE ANALYSIS — succession and powers
4. BENEFICIARY ANALYSIS — who gets what and when
5. FUNDING STATUS — what is and is not in the trust
6. ISSUES — numbered, with severity
7. RECOMMENDATIONS
8. QUESTIONS FOR CLIENT
